Master’s thesis · National Central University

A Graph-based Approach for PM2.5 Prediction

Supervisor · Professor Min-Te Sun

Research role Primary researcher

Responsible for the end-to-end research and experimental work.

The research proposed an end-to-end PM2.5 forecasting system covering data preprocessing, data fusion, feature engineering, feature selection, and multistep prediction.

I led the research design, data preparation, model development, experiment execution, evaluation, and analysis. The DCRNN-GS model combined DCRNN for spatio-temporal dependencies with GraphSAGE for learning meaningful information across sensor nodes. It achieved a 5.66% MSE improvement over the compared state-of-the-art approaches for forecasting the next 24 hours from the previous 24 hours.
